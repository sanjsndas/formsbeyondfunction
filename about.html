    Forma & Light

    Independent editorial · Est. 2025

    Forma & Light is an independent editorial publication dedicated to the art and craft of interior design and architecture. We explore the spaces we inhabit—from the smallest detail of a door handle to the sweeping gesture of a cantilevered roof—with curiosity, rigour, and a deep respect for material honesty.

    Founded in 2025 by architect and writer Elena Marchetti, our journal exists to bridge the gap between professional practice and everyday living. We believe that good design is not a luxury reserved for glossy magazines; it is a fundamental part of how we experience daily life. A well-proportioned room, a thoughtfully placed window, a material that ages gracefully—these are the quiet heroes of a life well lived.

    Our Mission

    We publish long-form guides, practical knowledge, and critical essays for design-conscious readers—architects, interior designers, self-builders, renovators, and anyone who cares about the spaces they occupy. Our editorial approach is simple: we favour depth over speed, substance over style, and clarity over jargon.

    Every article is written to be useful. Whether you are planning a kitchen renovation, specifying lighting for a living room, or simply trying to understand why a particular room feels so good to be in, we aim to provide the knowledge and confidence to make better decisions.

    What We Cover

    • Interior Design — principles, trends, and timeless approaches to furnishing and finishing interiors.
    • Architecture — residential and civic projects, facade design, and the poetics of built form.
    • Materials — stone, timber, lime plaster, concrete, and the honest expression of surfaces.
    • Lighting — layered schemes, colour temperature, and the interplay of natural and artificial light.
    • Space Planning — circulation, proportion, sightlines, and making the most of every square metre.
    • Sustainable Design — passive strategies, low-impact materials, and building for longevity.

    Editorial Independence

    Forma & Light is entirely independent. We do not accept sponsored content, and we do not allow advertisers to influence our editorial decisions. When we recommend a material, a product, or a practice, it is because we believe it is genuinely worth your attention—not because someone paid us to say so.

    “The best interiors are not those that look new forever, but those that grow more beautiful with use.”

    Meet the Editor

    EM
    Elena Marchetti

    Architect and design writer based in Milan. Elena holds a Master’s in Architecture from Politecnico di Milano and has over a decade of experience in residential and adaptive-reuse projects across Europe. She founded Forma & Light to explore the intersection of material culture, light, and daily life.

    Our Values

    • Clarity — We explain complex ideas in plain language, without dumbing them down.
    • Integrity — Our recommendations are independent and evidence-based.
    • Depth — We take the time to research thoroughly and write thoughtfully.
    • Beauty — We believe that visual and material beauty enriches human experience.
