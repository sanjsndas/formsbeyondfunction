    Cookies Policy

    Last updated 15 January 2025

    This Cookies Policy explains how Forma & Light ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on www.formaandlight.com (the "Site"). It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control our use of them.

    If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at privacy@formaandlight.com.

    1. What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners.

    Cookies can be "persistent" or "session" cookies. Persistent cookies remain on your device when you go offline, while session cookies are deleted as soon as you close your web browser.

    2. How We Use Cookies

    We use cookies for several reasons:

    • Essential cookies: Necessary for the operation of the Site (e.g., remembering your cookie consent preference).
    • Performance & analytics: To collect information about how visitors use the Site, such as which pages are visited most often and if users get error messages. These help us improve the Site.
    • Functionality: To remember choices you make (such as your language or region) and provide enhanced, more personal features.
    • Targeting / advertising: We do not currently use advertising cookies, but may in the future. If we do, we will update this policy and request consent where required.

    Detailed cookie table

    Below is a list of the specific cookies we may use, along with their purpose and duration.

    Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
    cookie_consent Essential Stores your cookie consent preference so we don't ask again. 1 year
    _ga Analytics (Google) Used by Google Analytics to distinguish users and track site usage. 2 years
    _gid Analytics (Google) Used by Google Analytics to distinguish users; expires after 24 hours. 24 hours
    _gat Analytics (Google) Used to throttle request rate for Google Analytics. 1 minute
    mailchimp_* Functionality Set by Mailchimp when you interact with a newsletter form to ensure functionality. Varies (up to 1 year)

    We also use third-party services such as Google Fonts and Unsplash for displaying fonts and imagery; these services may set their own cookies. We do not control those cookies, and you should review the respective third-party policies for more information.

    3. Third-Party Cookies

    In addition to our own cookies, we may also use various third-party cookies to report usage statistics of the Site, deliver advertisements (if applicable), and so on. These third parties may include:

    • Google Analytics
    • Mailchimp (newsletter)
    • Social media platforms (if you interact with sharing buttons)

    Please refer to the privacy and cookie policies of these third parties for more information. We do not control these cookies.

    4. Your Choices Regarding Cookies

    You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by:

    • Browser settings: Most web browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you disable cookies, some parts of the Site may not function properly.
    • Cookie consent banner: When you first visit our Site, you will see a banner asking for your consent to non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the Site, or by using the link in the footer (if available).

    To find out more about how to manage and delete cookies, visit www.aboutcookies.org or www.allaboutcookies.org.

    5. Changes to This Cookies Policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. We will post the updated version on this page with a new "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
