    Terms of Privacy

    Last updated: April 2025

    Forma & Light — independent editorial on interior design, architecture, materials, space planning, lighting and modern living. Your privacy and trust are important to us. This page explains how we handle your information and the terms under which you may use our website and content.

    Welcome to Forma & Light ("we", "us", "our"). This Terms of Privacy page governs your use of our website, www.formaandlight.com, and any related services, newsletters, or content (collectively, the "Site"). By accessing or using the Site, you agree to be bound by these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

    1. Information We Collect

    We collect minimal information to operate our editorial website and deliver our newsletter. Specifically:

    • Information you provide voluntarily: When you subscribe to our newsletter, you provide your email address. We use this solely to send you editorial updates. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.
    • Automatically collected information: Like most websites, we may collect non-personal data such as browser type, device type, referring pages, and pages visited. This data is aggregated and used to understand readership trends and improve our content. We do not use invasive tracking or sell data to third parties.
    • Cookies: We use only essential cookies to ensure the Site functions properly. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. See our Cookies Policy for details.

    2. How We Use Your Information

    We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

    • To send our newsletter to subscribers who have opted in.
    • To analyze aggregate site usage so we can improve our editorial content and user experience.
    • To respond to inquiries sent via our contact page.
    • To comply with legal obligations, if applicable.

    We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not run third-party advertisements on the Site.

    3. Editorial Independence & Advertising

    Forma & Light is an independent editorial publication. We do not accept sponsored content, and we do not allow advertisers to influence our editorial decisions. Any product recommendations or mentions are based on genuine editorial merit. This independence is central to our mission and to your trust.

    4. Data Security

    We take reasonable measures to protect the limited personal information we hold (such as email addresses). Our newsletter list is stored securely, and access is restricted. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to protecting your data to the best of our ability.

    5. Third-Party Links

    Our Site may contain links to external websites (e.g., reference sources, image credits, or related reading). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

    6. Your Rights

    Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data, including:

    • The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
    • The right to correct inaccurate data.
    • The right to request deletion of your data (e.g., unsubscribe from our newsletter and have your email removed).
    • The right to object to processing or request restriction.

    To exercise any of these rights, please contact us via our contact page. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

    7. Children's Privacy

    Our content is intended for a general audience interested in design and architecture. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

    8. Changes to This Terms of Privacy Page

    We may update this page from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top will indicate when the latest revision was made. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
